# Incident Response Process

## Roles

Incident commander, communications lead, scribe.

## Process

1. Declare the incident in #incidents.
2. Assign severity using the severity guide.
3. Post status updates every 30 minutes for SEV1.
4. Write a postmortem within five business days.

Infrastructure changes go through Terraform plans that need two approvals. Postmortems are blameless and are published within five business days.
