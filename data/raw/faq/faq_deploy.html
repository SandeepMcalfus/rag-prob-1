Deployment FAQ

How do deployments work?

We use GitOps: CI bumps the image tag in the Helm chart values and ArgoCD syncs the change.

Can I deploy on Fridays?

Low-risk changes only, and only before the freeze window.

How do I roll back?

Use ArgoCD history to roll back to the previous synced revision.
